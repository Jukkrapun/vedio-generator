# Webtoon / Clean-Line Cartoon Style: Audience, Mood, Short-Form Uses

## Webtoon market size/readership in Thailand & Asia, demographics

### Takeaway
Thailand is one of WEBTOON's main SEA markets: about 3.5M MAU as of 2022, with 100+ Thai creators. Asia-Pacific is the largest region for webtoons, though market-size estimates vary widely between firms. Readers skew 18–33, but I found no Thailand-specific age data.

### Cited Findings
- LINE WEBTOON Thailand had over 3.5M MAU (March 2022). Readership grew 33% from 2020, to as many as 172M views/month. Paid users rose ~30% from 2020 to 2021. It works with 100+ Thai professional creators, and some Thai titles have been exported. This is a sponsored/PR piece. — [Bangkok Post PR](https://www.bangkokpost.com/thailand/pr/2376128/webtoon-ecosystem-established-by-line-webtoon-is-turning-into-a-big-industry); [The people behind hit content (PR)](https://www.bangkokpost.com/thailand/pr/2364624/the-people-behind-the-success-of-hit-content-on-line-webtoon)
- The company itself claims (citing data.ai) to be Thailand's #1 webcomic platform by revenue and MAU. — [Naver press release](https://www.navercorp.com/en/media/pressReleasesDetail?seq=31229); [WEBTOON press](https://about.webtoon.com/press-release/101)
- Global webtoon market estimates for 2025 are ~USD 10.85B (Mordor, with APAC at 60.45%) and USD 11.72B (TechSci, with APAC the largest region). Persistence puts APAC at only ~30.9%, which conflicts with Mordor. Expert Market Research puts APAC at USD 1.61B in 2024, while IMARC puts it at USD 9.17B in 2024. The spread is very wide. — [Mordor](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/webtoons-market); [Persistence](https://www.persistencemarketresearch.com/market-research/webtoons-market.asp); [IMARC](https://www.imarcgroup.com/webtoons-market-statistics); [TechSci](https://www.techsciresearch.com/report/webtoons-market/29662.html)
- Korean government survey: domestic webtoon revenue was 2.286T won in 2024, up 4.4%. — [UPI](https://www.upi.com/Entertainment_News/2025/12/29/webtoons/1821767013512/)
- A 2025 Journal of Documentation study (not Thailand-specific) found 76% of Webtoon readers are aged 18–33. — [USF / J. Documentation](https://digitalcommons.usf.edu/si_facpub/650)
- Device-signal data says 56.9% of Thai "comic book enthusiasts" are 18–24. This is a weak proxy. — [Start.io](https://www.start.io/audience/comic-book-enthusiasts-in-thailand)
- In a 2023 multi-country survey, ~54% of Thai respondents said they would pay for Korean webtoons in future. — [Statista](https://statista.com/statistics/1254951/south-korea-korean-webtoon-intention-to-consume-paid-webtoons-in-selected-countries)

### Inferences
- The core webtoon audience (18–33) overlaps with the TikTok/Reels audience for late-night relationship and work content. Thai viewers are already familiar with the webtoon visual language.

### Gaps
- No Thai MAU figures after 2022 were found, and none since the rebrand to "WEBTOON".
- No Thai gender/age breakdown for webtoon readers (the Statista data is paywalled).

## Clean-line cartoon accounts on short-form; what makes them perform

### Takeaway
Relatable comic and illustration accounts grow by capturing a specific everyday or emotional moment in a simple, readable drawing. Webtoon platforms are now turning comics into vertical short video to reach Gen Z, and the industry sees TikTok/Reels/Shorts as its main competitor.

### Cited Findings
- Choco & Pancake (couple-relationship comics) has 780k+ followers on Instagram and nearly 1M on TikTok. — [Makeship](https://blog.makeship.com/interview/creator-catch-up-choco-and-pancake)
- An illustrator posting relatable dating/insecurity drawings had 15k followers on IG but 154.2k on TikTok, so TikTok gave far more reach. — [Creative Bloq](https://www.creativebloq.com/design/social-media/9-social-media-tips-for-illustrators-graphic-designers-and-creatives)
- Comic creators report that TikTok reaches audiences outside the comics-industry bubble, and that process/live-drawing videos get high engagement. — [The Long Box](https://thelongbox.substack.com/p/the-rise-and-rise-of-tiktok-comic)
- Women illustrators who draw anxiety and imperfection grew as a reaction against Instagram's "cult of perfection", so vulnerability drives the relatability. — [Stylist](https://stylist.co.uk/life/rise-instagram-female-illustrator-cartoonist-anxiety-relatable-funny-jessie-cave-gemma-correll-siobhan-gallagher-sally-nixon/65027)
- WEBTOON is producing vertical short-form adaptations that mix still panels with partial animation, voice, SFX and music. The first batch covered 14 comics. The goal is to reach Gen Z who prefer watching to reading, and creator Jessica Ramsden calls them "webcomics' version of anime". — [Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2025/08/18/webtoon-webcomics-anime-short-form-vertical-adaptations/)
- WEBTOON is testing 280 shorts (20 per franchise). — [Kidscreen](https://kidscreen.com/?p=225292); [MIDiA](https://www.midiaresearch.com/blog/why-webtoons-short-form-video-drive-is-a-lesson-in-format-flexibility)
- Naver has launched "Cuts" and Kakao "Helix Shorts" (AI that summarizes webtoons into short video). An industry insider calls Shorts/TikTok/Reels webtoons' biggest competitors. — [Korea Times](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/amp/lifestyle/trends/20250903/korean-webtoon-giants-turn-comics-into-videos-to-capture-short-form-generation); [Daum](https://v.daum.net/v/20250806132501530)
- Thai examples: Sundae Kids (romance moments taken from songs, quotes and friends' lives) has 1.4M+ Facebook followers. Tum Ulit draws bittersweet comics with a twist ending. Tuna Dunn draws cute comics about crushes and heartbreak with a dark edge. — [Khaosod](https://www.khaosodenglish.com/featured/2019/02/04/show-of-love-webcomic-sundae-kids-leaps-to-life-in-bangkok/); [Bored Panda](https://boredpanda.com/illustration-bittersweet-comics-tum-ulit); [Vice](https://www.vice.com/en/article/tuna-dunn-thai-illustrator-makes-her-comics-exclusively-in-english/)
- The history of motion comics (e.g. Warner's Watchmen) shows mixed reception. — [Wikipedia](https://www.Wikipedia.com/wiki/Motion_comic)

### Inferences
- What succeeds is a "specific moment + short line + simple drawing" format, which matches ตอนตีสอง's quote format. Successful Thai pages (Sundae Kids, Tum Ulit) are built on bittersweet emotion rather than polished visuals.
- The still-panel + slow camera + voice format already matches the formula WEBTOON itself uses.

### Gaps
- No completion-rate or retention data was found comparing illustrated vs photoreal clips.
- No current follower counts for Thai pages (the figures above are from 2019).

## How clean-line/flat style conveys late-night melancholy

### Takeaway
The recurring techniques are a limited palette (blue/indigo/muted), negative space, soft light, and one warm accent such as a lamp or a phone screen. Sources are art blogs and moodboards, not research.

### Cited Findings
- Start from a limited palette and a single emotional tension, then add negative space, soft light and recurring symbols. — [Odintsova](https://kseniaodintsova.com/blogs/tales/melancholic-aesthetic-colours-symbols-and-visual-themes)
- Blue connotes distance, reflection and loss. Melancholy comes through muted tones (grey, faded violet) better than bright colors. — [Odintsova](https://kseniaodintsova.com/blogs/tales/melancholy-in-color-blues-greens-and-muted-palettes-in-art)
- Night palettes often pair deep blues with a few vivid accents so the image isn't flat black. — [Linearity](https://www.linearity.io/blog/dark-color-palette)
- Negative space and dimmed colors can convey the spirit of night. — [Dribbble](https://WWW.dribbble.com/shots/15217498-Vampire)

### Inferences
- For ตอนตีสอง: navy/indigo base + one warm point (lamp, phone screen, convenience-store light) + a lot of empty space in the frame. Empty space also leaves room for subtitles in the safe zone.

### Gaps
- No research directly measured emotion from color temperature in cartoon short video.

## Comparison vs chibi and semi-realistic anime

### Takeaway
Chibi reads well at small sizes and suits humor, but tends to undercut serious emotion. Semi-real/anime faces carry subtle emotion better in close-up. Clean-line webtoon sits in between. Backlash against "AI slop" is strong (notably the Ghibli-AI backlash), and polished generic AI looks are the ones that draw it.

### Cited Findings
- Chibi exaggerates emotional reactions for comic effect. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chibi_(style))
- Chibi stays legible at small sizes (stickers, gacha), but is not recommended for close-up portraits where the face must carry the feeling. A head-and-shoulders anime portrait reads better in a small frame. Note this is an SEO-style source. — [Oakgen](https://oakgen.ai/blog/chibi-vs-anime-vs-cartoon-style)
- One critic argues that chibi's cuteness "cheats the viewer of an emotional payoff" in serious moments. — [Deracine](https://deracine.substack.com/p/to-chibi-or-not-chibi)
- Use of the term "AI slop" rose 9x in 2025, and negative sentiment peaked at 54% (Oct 2025). The March 2025 Ghibli-AI backlash came from a demand for authenticity. These are vendor figures. — [Meltwater](https://meltwater.com/en/blog/ai-slop-consumer-sentiment-social-listening-analysis)
- Slop conversations in art and culture rose 125% in 2025. Audiences reject work that feels "soulless". — [Brandwatch](https://www.brandwatch.com/blog/what-is-ai-slop/)
- Brands (Valentino, McDonald's) pulled AI ads after audiences called them flat and out of touch. — [McGraw Hill blog 2026](https://www.mheducation.com/highered/blog/2026/05/ai-slop-backfires-in-ads); [Digiday](https://digiday.com/marketing/in-graphic-detail-the-growing-rift-between-ai-efficiency-and-audience-trust)
- What counts as "slop" depends on the viewer. AI becomes slop when the audience can feel the cost-cutting more clearly than the idea. — [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2025/04/24/ai-slop-studio-ghibli-barbie-deepfakes)

### Inferences
- Clean-line webtoon style is a good fit for quiet late-night emotion. It is easier to read on a phone than semi-real, more serious than chibi, and it is easier to keep a character consistent because there are fewer details.
- Imitating Ghibli or the generic glossy AI look increases slop risk. A consistent house style (one palette, one recurring character) plus a sincere script reduces it.
- Production speed: flat style is likely faster to generate/fix and gives cleaner depth maps for parallax. This is my own inference, with no source.

### Gaps
- No controlled study compares retention or emotion by style (chibi vs webtoon vs semi-real) on phones.
- No data on how Thai audiences specifically perceive AI-made images.
