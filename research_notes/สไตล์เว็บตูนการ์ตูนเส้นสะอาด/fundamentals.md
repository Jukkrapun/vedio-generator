# Webtoon and Clean-Line Cartoon Style: Fundamentals and Character Design

## Definition & history (Korean webtoon, Thai scene, ligne claire, modern flat)

### Takeaway
Webtoon is a Korean web/mobile comic form defined by **vertical scrolling**. Daum launched first in 2003 and Naver followed in 2004 or 2005; sources disagree on the date. LINE WEBTOON has offered a Thai service since Nov 2014 and leads the Thai market. Ligne claire ("clear line") is Hergé's Tintin style: uniform outlines, flat colour, little shading, the term coined in 1977. Modern flat illustration (Kurzgesagt-like) shares the "simplified shapes + solid colour" idea.

### Cited Findings
- Daum Webtoon started in 2003, and Naver Webtoon followed in 2004 — [Wikipedia: Webtoon (platform)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Webtoon_(platform)). Naver's corporate history dates its official launch to Dec 2005, but a 2014 company announcement said 2004 — same source. The V&A gives Daum 2004 and Naver 2005 — [V&A: A brief history of webtoons](https://vam.ac.uk/articles/a-brief-history-of-webtoons). The sources conflict.
- Authors only moved to a vertical scrolling layout once preloading improved — [Wikipedia: Webtoon](https://en.wikipedia.com/wiki/Webtoon). What made the webtoon significant was the form itself: it used the web's "scroll" view instead of turning pages — [Harvard D3 case study](https://d3.harvard.edu/platform-digit/submission/naver-webtoon-winning-through-content-crowdsourcing/index.html).
- Naver says it pioneered the mobile vertical-scroll web comic. Its Dec 2025 post marked 20 years since the official launch — [NAVER story](https://www.navercorp.com/en/story/storyDetail?seq=34253).
- WEBTOON has run a Thai-language service ("LINE WEBTOON") since November 2014 — [WEBTOON press release](https://about.webtoon.com/press-release/101).
- In Thailand LINE WEBTOON had over 3.5M MAU in March 2022, and data.ai ranks it #1 by revenue and MAU. It supports more than 100 Thai professional creators. A hit Thai title, *Wanthong*, has 27M views — [Bangkok Post](https://www.bangkokpost.com/thailand/pr/2376128/webtoon-ecosystem-established-by-line-webtoon-is-turning-into-a-big-industry).
- Producers critique Thai creators' visuals and storytelling, and creators can submit weekly for regular income — [Bangkok Post](https://www.bangkokpost.com/thailand/pr/2364624/the-people-behind-the-success-of-hit-content-on-line-webtoon); [ThaiPR](https://www.thaipr.net/?p=3219301).
- At LINE CONFERENCE THAILAND 2025, WEBTOON's SEA lead said the company would add 52 original Thai works and expand Thai works into other IP — [LY Corp](https://www.lycorp.co.jp/en/story/20250530/lineconference.html).
- Kakao Webtoon launched its Thai app in June 2021. One paper says it made 370M THB in its first four days — [JUCR, Chula](https://so04.tci-thaijo.org/index.php/JUCR/article/download/281233/188077/1203732).
- Ligne claire facts:
  - Hergé pioneered it for Tintin, and Joost Swarte coined the term in 1977 — [Wikipedia: Ligne claire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ligne_claire).
  - Edgar P. Jacobs and others in the Brussels school took it up, and Yves Chaland and others revived it in 1980s France — same source.
  - Its traits are strong lines of uniform weight, no hatching, flat colour, low contrast and minimal shading — [AIGA Eye on Design](https://eyeondesign.aiga.org/how-this-illustrator-made-a-career-from-his-love-of-herge/).
  - Characters are cartoonish while backgrounds are realistic and well researched — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ligne_claire); [Tintinologist forum](https://www.tintinologist.org/forums/msg.php?id=44813).
  - The style aims at easy-to-read storytelling — [Larousse](https://www.larousse.fr/encyclopedie/personnage/wd/123707).
- Kurzgesagt describes its own look as vector illustration plus pixel illustration plus infographics, making a "distinctive playful style" — [Kurzgesagt](https://kurzgesagt.org/youtube/).
- Flat style relies on simplified shapes and solid colours with minimal detail. It avoids textures, gradients and perspective rendering, builds on basic geometry, and should be applied as one consistent style — [Linearity flat illustration guide](https://www.linearity.io/blog/flat-illustration-guide); [IBM Design flat style](https://www.ibm.com/design/language/illustration/flat-style/design).

### Inferences
- "Clean-line webtoon" for this channel is best read as a hybrid: ligne-claire-like even outlines and flat fills, applied to webtoon-style faces and proportions.

## Visual conventions (line weight, flat colour, cel shading, faces, palettes, vertical format vs 9:16)

### Takeaway
Ligne claire uses **uniform** line weight. Webtoon production favours simple or no shading and flat base colours, because every panel has to be redrawn on a weekly schedule. Some top webtoons, such as Lore Olympus, lean on colour more than line. The native webtoon canvas, 800 px wide with 1280 px slices, is a 1:1.6 portrait. 9:16 is 1:1.78, so one webtoon slice is a little wider than a phone video frame.

### Cited Findings
- Webtoon simplification advice:
  - Use no shading or only simple shading.
  - Focus on linework, shade tones, textures and details that are easy to repeat in every panel.
  - Keep any outline effect close to the main line thickness and colour.
  — [Clip Studio: Simplifying Characters for Webtoons](https://www.clipstudio.net/how-to-draw/archives/166899)
- A single texture layer set to Color Burn gives a "textured paper" look — same source.
- Lore Olympus composes "almost exclusively with brilliant color" rather than heavy black lines, using minimal linework — [NPR review](https://www.npr.org/2021/10/27/1049434574/lore-olympus-rachel-smythe-review).
- Lore Olympus gives each character a signature colour (Persephone pink, Hades blue), chosen for storytelling — [Comics Beat interview](https://www.comicsbeat.com/interview-rachel-smythe-lore-olympus); [World History review](https://www.worldhistory.org/review/259/lore-olympus-volume-one/).
- Emotion in Lore Olympus is also carried by symbols and splashes of colour — [All About Romance](https://allaboutromance.com/book-review/lore-olympus-by-rachel-smythe/).
- Canvas sizes:
  - The WEBTOON upload maximum is 800×1280 px per image, and many artists work at 1600×2560 — [Clip Studio tips 7396](https://tips.clip-studio.com/en-us/articles/7396).
  - Some artists draw at 1600 px wide and export at 800 — [Clip Studio tips 9420](https://tips.clip-studio.com/ko-kr/articles/9420?org=1).
- Spacing:
  - Leave at least 200 px between panels, and 600–1000 px for a scene or time transition (on an 800 px width) — [WEBTOON Creators Resource Handbook](https://webtoons-static.pstatic.net/creator101/en/pdf/Creators-Resource-Handbook-Updated.pdf); [Clip Studio CANVAS article](https://tips.clip-studio.com/zh-tw/articles/4891?org=1).
  - An informal rule of thumb is gutters of about 800 px (standard) and about 400 px (continuing dialogue) — [Content Curve](https://contentcurve.substack.com/p/introduction-to-webtoon-paneling).

### Inferences
- The webtoon reading rhythm maps onto a 9:16 video's time axis. Big gutters work like pauses or cuts, and one panel per screen works like one shot, which fits a one-image-per-segment pipeline.
- To keep ligne-claire cleanliness, prompts should ask for "uniform clean outlines, flat colours, at most one cel-shadow tone".

### Gaps
- I found no source with concrete palette statistics for webtoons (saturation, value ranges).

## Proportions & face design (romance/slice-of-life webtoon vs clean-line cartoon)

### Takeaway
Romance and slice-of-life webtoons use semi-realistic anatomy with enlarged, expressive eyes and small noses, and the skull stays close to real human structure. True Beauty is described as semi-realistic and delicate, with subtle expressions. Ligne claire instead puts cartoonish, simplified faces on realistic backgrounds.

### Cited Findings
- Big expressive eyes carry the emotion. Exaggeration starts from real proportions, and romance rarely uses a gritty or hyper-real style — [Clip Studio: developing style](https://www.clipstudio.net/en/comics-manga/developing-style/); [Geek Vibes Nation](https://geekvibesnation.com/manhwa-art-style/).
- Keep the skull consistent with human measurements even when the eyes are big and the nose small — [Creative Bloq: manga faces](https://creativebloq.com/how-to/manga-faces).
- Yaongyi's style in True Beauty and Return Your Soul is "semi-realistic" — [ComicBook.com](https://comicbook.com/anime/news/true-beauty-new-webtoon-yaongyi).
- True Beauty's beauty is "painted very delicately", with subtle expressions — [Webtoon Guide review](https://www.webtoonguide.com/ko/board/Review_en/10289).
- Some critics find the look generic: one compared it to "freemium shovelware phone games" — [Anime News Network](https://Animenewsnetwork.Com/review/true-beauty-manhwa/volume-1-3/.205459).
- Lore Olympus's style drifted over the run, and Persephone became less extreme over time — [All About Romance](https://allaboutromance.com/book-review/lore-olympus-by-rachel-smythe/).

### Inferences
- For ใบเตย (Baitoey), a semi-real webtoon face suits the late-night romance tone: real skull, larger eyes, minimal nose.
- Ligne claire fits better for a mascot such as ส้มฉุน (Somchun).

### Gaps
- I found no published head-to-body ratios or eye-placement numbers for romance webtoons. Commonly cited figures (about 7–8 heads for adults) are unsourced here.

## Best practices, common mistakes, recognizability, production pipeline

### Takeaway
A recognizable character comes from a readable silhouette, a few defining features, a fixed colour set and a model sheet. The standard pipeline (sketch, inks, flats on a separate layer, shading clipped to the flats) is efficient because each stage is non-destructive and can be repeated.

### Cited Findings
- Recognizability:
  - Choose a few defining features and avoid intricate armour or accessories that are slow to redraw.
  - Save colour sets and brushes, and write a character description so the design stays consistent.
  — [Clip Studio: Simplifying Characters](https://www.clipstudio.net/how-to-draw/archives/166899)
  - Test the silhouette as a solid black shape, and use shape language (circle = friendly, square = stable, triangle = danger) — [BINUS DKV](https://binus.ac.id/bandung/dkv/?p=971).
  - Model sheets keep work unified across artists, and their construction lines hold proportions steady across views — [Wikipedia: Model sheet](https://Www.wikipedia.org/wiki/Model_sheet); [Merritt-Houghton substack](https://cmerritthoughton.substack.com/p/a-guide-to-character-design-sheets).
  - Test a design with a neutral face, a 3/4 view and a wide scene — [Clip Studio tips 8662](https://tips.clip-studio.com/en-us/articles/8662).
- AI generation (vendor sources, weigh accordingly):
  - Expressions such as shouting or surprise can make models change the jawline and eyes, so build sheets in stages.
  - A distinctive silhouette anchors identity.
  — [Kapwing](https://www.kapwing.com/resources/how-to-create-a-character-reference-sheet-with-ai/); [Vidu](https://www.vidu.com/blog/character-design-ai-video)
- Pipeline:
  - Keep flats on a separate layer from the inks; this is faster and non-destructive — [Clip Studio tips 7895](https://tips.clip-studio.com/en-us/articles/7895).
  - The colour chosen at the flats stage doesn't matter; what matters is that the areas are separated — [Clip Studio tips 800](https://tips.clip-studio.com/en-us/articles/800).
  - Set the flats layer as the reference layer — [Clip Studio tips 3771](https://tips.clip-studio.com/zh-tw/articles/3771?org=1).
  - Paint shadows in ONE colour on a layer clipped to the flats — [Clip Studio tips 2963](https://tips.clip-studio.com/fr-fr/articles/2963?org=1).
  - Define the light and shadow areas right after the linework, then fill-tool the cel shading — [Clip Studio tips 6331](https://tips.clip-studio.com/fr-fr/articles/6331?org=1).
  - Common blend modes are Multiply, Overlay, Color Burn and Screen — [Clip Studio tips 5559](https://tips.clip-studio.com/ja-jp/articles/5559?org=1).
- Mistakes the Clip Studio article implies:
  - Outline thickness that doesn't match the rest of the linework.
  - Soft brushes that bleed into the edges.
  - Untested textures.
  — [Clip Studio: Simplifying Characters](https://www.clipstudio.net/how-to-draw/archives/166899)

### Inferences
- For AI image generation: attach a model sheet, which the repo's `image_refs` already supports. Lock 2–3 signature features plus a fixed palette in `image_style`, avoid busy accessories, and keep the shading to one tone. This mirrors the manual pipeline's sources of consistency.
