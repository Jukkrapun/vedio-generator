# Watercolor & Hand-Drawn Illustration: Style and Character Design Fundamentals

Note on sources: most findings below come from search-result summaries of tutorials and blogs (Skillshare, Jackson's, Dick Blick, Clip Studio, Creative Bloq, Wacom). Only the Creative Boom article on Puuung was fetched in full. Treat technique claims as studio practice that many sources share, not as authoritative references.

## Sub-styles of the watercolor / hand-drawn family

### Takeaway
Traditional watercolor gets its look from how water, timing, pigment and paper interact: wet-on-wet, glazing, blooms and granulation. Gouache is the opaque, matte relative. Emotional Korean couple illustration (Puuung) is mostly painterly digital work styled like home movies, not literal watercolor.

### Cited Findings
- Wet-on-wet means putting paint onto wet paper so colors flow and soften into each other. The working window is short. — [Udemy blog](https://blog.udemy.com/watercolor-painting-techniques/); [Artchive](https://www.artchive.com/art-mediums/watercolor/techniques/)
- Blooms (also called backruns or cauliflowers) form when water or wet paint meets a drying wash, or when different areas dry at different speeds. They leave uneven shapes with hard edges. — [Udemy blog](https://blog.udemy.com/watercolor-painting-techniques/)
- Glazing means laying thin transparent color over dry color. It works best with transparent pigments, because cheaper paints can turn muddy when layered. — [Skillshare](https://www.skillshare.com/en/blog/essential-watercolor-techniques-for-every-artist/)
- Granulation is pigment settling unevenly into the paper's texture. It depends on the pigment and the paper, especially rough paper, more than on technique. — [Artchive](https://www.artchive.com/art-mediums/watercolor/techniques/); [Getty](https://www.getty.edu/art/exhibitions/prismatic/watercolor_techniques.html)
- Watercolor is luminous because light passes through the pigment and bounces off the white paper. Gouache is opaque and dries matte: its denser particles, often with added chalk or titanium white, scatter light at the surface. — [Dick Blick](https://www.dickblick.com/learning-resources/how-to/gouache-vs-watercolor/); [Jackson's](https://www.jacksonsart.com/blog/?p=1516592)
- Gouache played a major role in mid-century illustration and animation. A common hybrid is to lay in watercolor washes, then add gouache for opaque highlights and details once the washes are fully dry. — [Skillshare](https://www.skillshare.com/en/blog/gouache-vs-watercolor-when-and-how-to-use-each-medium/); [Dick Blick](https://www.dickblick.com/learning-resources/how-to/gouache-vs-watercolor/)
- Puuung is the pen name of Park Da-mi, a South Korean illustrator and animator. Since 2014 the "Love Is" series has shown small everyday moments of a couple. It was featured in the 2016 K-drama *W: Two Worlds*. — [Korea.net](https://www.korea.net/NewsFocus/People/view?articleId=147309); [My Modern Met](https://mymodernmet.com/puuung-love-is)
- Puuung's world is described as "painterly and rather Parisian", like 8mm home movies (compared to an animated *Amélie*). The themes are "normal daily lives that are nothing special" and "love shines in these small moments". The work has little dialogue. Puuung mentions painting "on the screen", which points to digital work. — [Creative Boom](https://www.creativeboom.com/inspiration/puung/)
- Thai illustrators found in this family: Supawan Tayarachakul ("miminini") calls her style "detailed, whimsical watercolor line drawing" and made the storybook *Kids Have Dreams* — [art4d](https://art4d.com/en/2024/08/miminini). Ploypisut is a Thai freelance illustrator whose watercolor flora and fauna work extends into patterns — [Lake Coloring interview](https://blog.lakecoloring.com/interview-ploypisut/).

### Inferences
- For our AI pipeline, a "watercolor" prompt should name the physical cues: visible paper grain, soft wet-on-wet skies, bloom edges, granulating shadows, and white paper left as highlights. Without them, models drift toward smooth digital gradients.
- The Puuung-style emotional look is a warm, painterly digital look plus everyday intimacy. Borrow the mood and the subject matter, not the exact rendering. We should not imitate any named artist, including Ghibli, in prompts.

### Gaps
- No sources were found for ink and wash specifics, colored pencil or crayon, pencil-sketch styles, or Ghibli background technique, because searches ran out of budget.
- Puuung's exact software and brushes were not confirmed.
- No well-documented Thai watercolor storybook illustrator with major publications was found.

## Character design in watercolor

### Takeaway
Use simple, shape-based designs with a strong silhouette and a consistent turnaround. Use a soft outline (or none) and controlled edges. Keep heavy costume detail low, because washes swallow small detail.

### Cited Findings
- Picture-book character design relies on shapes to convey personality. Simpler lines and basic shapes are favored over heavy costume detail. — [Skillshare, Mirka Hokkanen Pt 1](https://www.skillshare.com/en/classes/character-design-for-picture-books-part-1/837508819)
- Recognisable characters can be read from a quick glimpse of their silhouette. A design should mix large, medium and small shapes. — [Creative Bloq](https://www.creativebloq.com/art/character-design/how-to-design-instantly-recognisable-characters-using-strong-silhouettes)
- Make a turnaround so the character stays consistent. — [Skillshare, Sandra Mejia](https://www.skillshare.com/en/classes/part-1-a-characters-journey-designing-cute-characters-in-procreate/260254629)
- Watercolor character workflow: draw a soft outline first, then lay in washes. — [Skillshare, Mary D](https://www.skillshare.com/en/classes/cute-and-simple-characterdesign-from-sketch-to-watercoloring/729223722)
- Use edges deliberately: keep them hard where you want attention and soft where you want areas to recede. — [Eckersleys](https://www.eckersleys.com.au/blogs/learn/7-common-watercolour-mistakes-and-how-to-avoid-them)
- The white of the paper is precious in transparent watercolor. Save it for highlights. — [Outdoor Painter, Brenda Swenson](https://www.outdoorpainter.com/?p=29111)

### Inferences
- Lock in a few signature traits (hair shape, one accessory, one color) so the character survives loose washes from image to image. Attaching a reference sheet, as in the cat-2am POC, fits this approach.
- Choosing between line and lineless: ink or pencil lines keep identity steadier across AI images, while lineless pure-wash work drifts more.

### Gaps
- No primary source compared line vs lineless for consistency, and none gave rules for limited palettes in character design.

## Quiet / nostalgic / late-night mood

### Takeaway
Build darkness from deep blues and violets rather than black. Keep the values lighter than feels natural. Get the lamp glow from preserved paper or a warm light wash set against cool surroundings, using negative painting and glazing.

### Cited Findings
- Start lighter than feels right. Avoid black, which is "opaque, flat, and kills the glow". Use deep blues and violets for night. — [Jenna Rainey](https://jennarainey.com/?p=20402)
- Lanterns can glow even when the surrounding values are not very dark. A suggested limited palette is ultramarine, burnt sienna and a warm yellow. — [Watercolour Workout](https://watercolourworkout.substack.com/p/glow-in-the-dark)
- Night cityscapes rely on transparency and glazing to build luminosity. — [watercolorpainting.com, Tokyo night](https://watercolorpainting.com/watercolor-tokyo-night-cityscape/)
- Negative painting (painting around shapes) creates depth. — [Skillshare, Mirka Hokkanen](https://www.skillshare.com/en/classes/simplifying-watercolor-painting-techniques-for-negative-painting-and-creating-depth/770099090)
- Night-illustration atmosphere and lighting guidance is also available for digital work in Clip Studio. — [Clip Studio Tips](https://tips.clip-studio.com/en-us/articles/11478)

### Inferences
- For the ตอนตีสอง brand, a prompt recipe could be "indigo/violet night washes, a single warm amber lamp glow left from bare paper, cool-warm contrast, soft lost edges in the shadows".

## Common mistakes and handmade vs digital fake

### Takeaway
Mud comes from damp-layer reworking, opaque pigments, too many mixed colors and dirty water. Fake digital watercolor gives itself away with no paper texture, no edge darkening and uniform edges.

### Cited Findings
- Muddy color comes from painting into a damp layer (only paint onto paper that is fully dry or very wet), opaque paints, mixing many pigments, and dirty water. — [Eckersleys](https://www.eckersleys.com.au/blogs/learn/7-common-watercolour-mistakes-and-how-to-avoid-them); [Little Coffee Fox](https://littlecoffeefox.com/watercolor-mistakes/)
- Overworking destroys transparency. Aim to finish a shape in one or two passes, and let each layer dry fully. — [Jenna Rainey](https://jennarainey.com/5-common-watercolor-flower-mistakes-and-how-to-fix-them/); [Eckersleys](https://www.eckersleys.com.au/blogs/learn/7-common-watercolour-mistakes-and-how-to-avoid-them)
- Unwanted hard, dark rims come from pigment pooling in an overloaded wash as it dries. — [Louise De Masi](https://louise-demasi-4nkf.squarespace.com/tips/2024/10/31/ten-common-watercolour-mistakes-and-how-to-fix-them)
- To make digital art look like watercolor: use a real paper-texture photo blended with Color Burn, and imitate edge darkening with a thin Multiply inner glow. — [Creative Bloq](https://www.creativebloq.com/digital-art/make-your-digital-art-look-watercolour-3-easy-steps-121413744)
- Keep the paper layer at about 10–40% opacity, and allow imperfect, unblended edges. — [Clip Studio Tips](https://tips.clip-studio.com/en-us/articles/5599)
- Edge variation is one of the most overlooked techniques in digital painting. — [Wacom/Proko](https://community.wacom.com/en-au/how-to-make-digital-paintings-look-traditional)
- Users say watercolor looks fake without canvas texture, and that built-in edge darkening is too faint at high resolution. — [Adobe community](https://community.adobe.com/t5/fresco-discussions/canvas-textures-for-watercolor/td-p/11276171); [Escape Motions forum](https://www.escapemotions.com/community/forum/t/44574/a-question-about-edge-darkening-and-granulation)

### Inferences
- In the render pipeline, a subtle paper-grain overlay (about 10–30%) on AI watercolor images could make them feel more handmade. Check this so it doesn't fight the parallax.

### Gaps
- No studies were found on how viewers perceive AI-made watercolor as "fake".
