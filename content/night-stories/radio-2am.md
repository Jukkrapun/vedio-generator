# คลื่นตีสอง / 2 A.M. Radio: season plan

A recurring story set inside `night-stories` (user's topic, 2026-11-25). A woman who has not slept properly
for years takes the 2 a.m. slot that nobody wants at a small radio station. Each episode is one night on air.
Episodes are tagged `radio-2am`, and titles read "คลื่นตีสอง ตอนที่ N: …" / "2 A.M. Radio, Night N: …".

## Rules for every episode

- **Stands alone and still lulls people to sleep** (series.md bedtime format). Each night has its own small
  story (a listener's message, a call, a song request) that is closed inside the episode.
- **One soft serial thread** runs across the season: the lit window on the 7th floor across the street and
  the listener who writes from it. Each episode moves it forward by one small step and ends on a *gentle*
  open question, not a cliffhanger (story-hook-framework B, softened for bedtime).
- **Fixed opening and closing** (series branding):
  - Open: the red on-air light comes on at exactly 2 a.m.
  - Close: "คลื่นตีสอง ขอตัวลาไปก่อนนะ… ใครที่ยังตื่นอยู่ ไม่ต้องรีบหลับนะ ฉันอยู่ตรงนี้ จนกว่าเธอจะหลับ… หลับฝันดีนะ"
- Narrator = the DJ ("ฉัน"). The DJ is never named; listeners have nicknames only. People are shown
  from behind or out of focus; no readable text (no station names or on-air lettering).
- Same studio images can be reused across episodes (desk, mic, window view) to build a visual home;
  put them in each episode's `images/` (copy) so episodes stay self-contained.

## Season 1 (6 nights)

| # | Title | The night's story | Thread step |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | คืนแรก / The First Night | Her first night on air, talking into silence; no calls for hours. | 03:40, the first and only message: "ไฟห้องชั้นเจ็ดที่คุณเห็น คือห้องฉันเอง ขอบคุณที่อยู่เป็นเพื่อน". The light blinks twice, then goes off. *Who is it?* |
| 2 | จดหมายที่ไม่ได้ส่ง / The Letter | A listener asks her to read on air a letter to someone they never said goodbye to. | Window 7 writes: "เพลงสุดท้ายเมื่อคืน เขาก็ชอบ". *Who is "he"?* |
| 3 | คนขับแท็กซี่ / The Taxi Driver | A night taxi driver calls in live; he plays the show for passengers who cry in the back seat. | The driver says he once drove an old woman home to that building across the street. |
| 4 | เพลงที่แม่ชอบ / Mother's Song | A request for an old song a mother used to hum; she finds it in the station's dusty vinyl room. | In the vinyl room she finds an old program sheet: this 2 a.m. slot was run 30 years ago by a man who signed off the same way. |
| 5 | คืนที่ไฟไม่เปิด / The Night the Light Stayed Off | Quiet listeners send small messages to check on each other. | Window 7 is dark all night, and no message comes. She keeps talking anyway, for that one window. |
| 6 | เช้าวันแรก / The First Morning | After the show she crosses the street at dawn. | Window 7 is the widow of the old 2 a.m. DJ; she has listened to that slot every night for 30 years. The circle closes: someone was always awake for someone. |

Later seasons can reuse the station and bring in new listeners.
