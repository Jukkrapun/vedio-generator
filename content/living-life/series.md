# Series: living-life (ใบเตยเล่าชีวิต)

Part of the channel **ตอนตีสอง**: adult-life thoughts at 2 a.m. — loneliness, friends drifting
apart, comparing yourself, living alone, overthinking. Short clips for Reels / TikTok / Shorts.

## Format spec

| Item | Value |
|------|-------|
| Aspect ratio | 9:16 vertical |
| Resolution | 1080x1920 |
| Max duration | 90 seconds (hard limit) |
| Target duration | ~20 seconds (4 segments) |
| Language | Thai |

## Voice & host

- ElevenLabs **Hammy** (`MYZYcGOqrkYQcwDunXl7`), `eleven_v3`, fallback edge-tts `th-TH-PremwadeeNeural`.
- Host ใบเตย speaks as a friend sharing, not a coach. At most one v3 tag per segment.

## Tone

- Name the feeling, reframe gently, give ONE small doable takeaway, close warm.
- Never: medical/mental-health diagnosis, money advice, mocking real people.

## Clip structure

1. **Hook** (0–3s) 2. **Insight** 3. **Takeaway** 4. **Closing** (warm line)

## Visuals (image mode)

- Shared `image_style`: late-night cinematic film photography, dim warm lamp and soft window light, deep blue shadows with warm amber highlights, gentle grain, shallow depth of field, quiet and lonely but comforting mood.
- Night bedrooms, small apartments, windows, rain, empty streets. People from behind / out of focus; no readable text, no logos.
